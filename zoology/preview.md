### 12-Hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid
12-Hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid (12-HETE) is a derivative of the 20 carbon [polyunsaturated fatty acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyunsaturated_fatty_acid), [arachidonic acid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arachidonic_acid), containing a [hydroxyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroxyl) residue at carbon 12 and a 5Z,8Z,10E,14Z [cis–trans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cis–trans_isomerism) configuration (Z=cis, E=trans) in its four double bonds.
